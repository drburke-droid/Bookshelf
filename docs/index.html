1. Your book list

Your file stays on your computer. Nothing is uploaded.

Where do I get my Goodreads file?
  1. On the Goodreads website, open My Books.
  2. Choose Tools → Import and export.
  3. Click Export Library, then download goodreads_library_export.csv.

A StoryGraph export, or any CSV with a Title column, also works.

2. Which books

Most books to show. 0 shows them all.

3. The look

A scene sets the wall, shelves, lighting and colours together. Change any of them afterwards.
